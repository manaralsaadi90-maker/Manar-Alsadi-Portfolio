## Overview

An interactive AI marketing tool for the sports-nutrition brand Upfront that replaces a static webshop with a **guided, personalised customer journey**. Users enter their goal, body data, activity level and diet; the tool calculates a personalised daily protein and calorie target and uses AI to recommend relevant products with tailored explanations.

<Callout>
I was the sole creator and product owner, responsible for the full lifecycle: concept, marketing strategy, brand positioning, UX/UI, the technical build, AI integration, documentation and the final presentation. This was an AI-assisted build that I designed and directed.
</Callout>

## Problem

A standard webshop leaves customers to figure out which product fits their goals. The opportunity: turn product discovery into a personalised, engaging experience that guides the customer and improves relevance.

## Research

I benchmarked ten comparable products (MyFitnessPal, Freeletics, Noom, WHOOP, Nike Training Club and others) to understand patterns in personalisation and engagement, then defined scope and prioritised features using the MoSCoW model.

## Concept & Marketing Strategy

I positioned the tool as a **two-stage marketing funnel**: an AR face filter ("Fuel Your Best Self") for top-of-funnel awareness, leading into the AI coach for conversion and engagement, applying hyperpersonalisation and AI-driven customer-journey principles, with a brand character ("FRANK") matched to Upfront's honest, no-nonsense voice.

## Data & Logic

I extracted and structured a **57-product database** from PDF source material (name, flavour, price, protein, calories, timing), built goal-to-product mapping with dietary-exclusion filtering, and implemented a nutrition calculator using the **Mifflin-St Jeor** formula for BMR, TDEE and protein targets.

## Development

I built a responsive web MVP with a 4-step onboarding form, branded UI and results screen; engineered the AI prompts for brand-voiced advice; built a no-code chatbot in Flowise; and created the AR filter in Lens Studio. Bilingual NL/EN content served the Dutch market.

## Challenges & Solutions

Keeping AI output on-brand and accurate required careful prompt engineering and review. Structuring messy PDF data into a clean, queryable database took disciplined data work before any build.

## Results

Delivered a complete, working MVP plus two supporting prototypes (AI chatbot + AR filter), meeting every course requirement, scope, MVP, two prototypes, MoSCoW model, requirement list, weekly planning and a documented prompt history (~57 logged hours).

<Callout>
Honesty note: there are no public audience or conversion metrics, because the prototype wasn't publicly deployed. The achievement here is a fully functional MVP and two prototypes, built and documented end-to-end.
</Callout>

## Lessons Learned

How to own a digital product from concept to build, and how AI, used deliberately, lets one person deliver strategy, design, data and code together.
