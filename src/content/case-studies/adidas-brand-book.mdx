## Overview

A professional **12-spread brand book** translating Adidas's identity, values and positioning into cohesive visual storytelling across sport, culture and innovation.

<Callout>
Student concept project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Adidas. The design and content are my academic work.
</Callout>

## Research

I researched Adidas's mission, vision, values, audience and campaigns (Originals, Messi, collaborations) and translated those insights into content themes.

## Content Strategy

I developed a structured content plan for 12 spreads across themes, innovation, lifestyle, athletes, sustainability, and wrote marketing-focused copy aligned to the "Impossible is Nothing" positioning, structured across the customer journey.

## Design

I designed and built the full book in **Adobe InDesign**, applying a consistent typographic scale, colour system and layout principles, with AI-generated visuals supporting the concepts, and prepared print-ready files (bleed, export settings).

## Results

A visually consistent, professionally structured, print-ready brand book that combines storytelling, branding and visual communication.

## Lessons Learned

A brand book is a system, not a set of nice pages, consistency and clear rules are what make it usable.

## Technologies

Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, AI image tools (DALL·E / Firefly), Canva, Figma.
