## Overview

A healthcare-innovation concept using drone technology and AI-supported diagnostics to improve emergency response times, support healthcare professionals and increase survival rates in critical situations.

<Callout>
Role: Research Lead, Concept Developer & AI-Assisted Content Creator. I proposed the idea, led research, developed the concept, coordinated feedback and supported prototype visualization. Student concept project.
</Callout>

## Problem

Emergency response delays cost lives. The brief: design an innovative solution that gets diagnostics and support to patients faster.

## Research

I conducted desk research on robotics, AI and healthcare innovation, analysed academic articles, case studies and industry trends, and evaluated legal and ethical considerations including Dutch healthcare regulation (Wet BIG).

## Concept & Validation

I developed the Emergency Drone Robot concept and value proposition, then validated it through interviews with healthcare professionals, refining the prototype across iterations based on their recommendations.

## Foresight & Analysis

I designed a **Futures Wheel** analysis to assess long-term societal, ethical and business impacts, and assessed market demand and adoption opportunities across hospitals and emergency services.

## Marketing & Communication

I identified target audiences, developed positioning, and created visual communication materials and storyboards, using AI tools to generate prototype visuals and translate technical concepts into audience-friendly messages.

## Results

A validated healthcare-innovation concept supported by research and stakeholder feedback, professional documentation, strategic analysis and visual prototypes, demonstrating AI-accelerated research and innovation.

## Lessons Learned

Innovation is only credible when validated, talking to real healthcare professionals reshaped the concept.
