title: Systems Thinking & B2B Consulting Prep
---

## Overview

A strategic-consulting exercise: analyse a consumer brand as part of a wider market system, identify a strategic marketing issue, and prepare a B2B consulting proposition, including the right decision-makers and an outreach approach.

<Callout>
Honest framing: this was research and outreach *preparation*, not paid campaigns or client delivery. I describe it as B2B audience analysis and outreach, and the outcome is the deliverables and skills, not client results.
</Callout>

## Research & Systems Mapping

I conducted desk research on the brand and its industry, then built **system maps**, an Iceberg Model and a Causal Loop Diagram, to show how market, sustainability and commercial factors interconnect.

## Problem Formulation

I formulated opportunities, challenges and testable assumptions, several focused on sustainability and circular-economy themes, using honest epistemic labelling ("plausible based on desk research" vs. "requires company confirmation").

## Stakeholder & DMU Analysis

I mapped the company's Decision-Making Unit, decision-makers, influencers and end users, and identified real, named contacts through research.

## B2B Outreach Toolkit

I developed a contact strategy linking DMU roles to specific assumptions, created **SPIN-based** sales question sets for each role, and wrote tailored LinkedIn and email outreach scripts.

## Results

A complete, submission-ready strategic-consulting report and presentation, plus a full B2B outreach toolkit (DMU map, SPIN questions, outreach scripts).

<Callout>
Honesty note: the outreach did not receive responses, so there are no measurable business results. The value here is the analysis, the toolkit and the applied skills.
</Callout>

## Lessons Learned

Systems thinking reframes a "marketing problem" as a set of connected causes, and honest labelling of what you know vs. assume builds trust.

## Technologies

LinkedIn, email, Word, Excel, APA research methods, systems-mapping/diagramming.
