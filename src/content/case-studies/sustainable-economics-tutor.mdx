## Overview

A self-contained study tool that turns a full Sustainable Economics course, six lectures plus five articles, into a single, reliable revision app for a 100% multiple-choice exam.

<Callout>
Role: project owner and quality reviewer. I defined requirements, supplied all source material, and critically checked each version, catching gaps and pushing for completeness rather than accepting approximations.
</Callout>

## Goal

Concepts explained simply, connected across lectures, and drilled through practice, all in one place.

## What I Built (directed)

An interactive HTML/JavaScript study application covering all six lectures with must-know / good-to-know tagging, a **30-term glossary**, **24 flashcards**, and a **39-question mock-quiz engine** with explanations and weak-area feedback.

## Quality & Fact-Checking

I reviewed drafts for accuracy and coverage and verified that source content was actually reflected, catching, for example, that the articles weren't fully incorporated and that the 17 SDGs weren't listed, then confirming article-accurate content like the planetary-boundaries timeline and the full SDG list.

## Results

A finished, verified, self-contained study tool, accurate to the source material and matched to the real exam format.

## Lessons Learned

The value I added wasn't the code, it was the critical review that insisted on completeness and accuracy.
