title: Voyanah, UX-Driven Travel Website
---

## Overview

A user-centered travel website that helps students and young adults discover, compare and understand affordable group trips across Europe, designed from research through to a live, functional build.

## Problem

Young travellers struggle to compare group trips quickly and confidently. Voyanah needed a clear structure and journey that made discovery and comparison effortless.

## Research

I used **card sorting** to define the navigation structure and wrote **user stories** to align features with what the target audience actually needed.

## UX & Wireframes

I designed low- and high-fidelity wireframes in Figma and built a **component-based design system** for consistency across pages.

## Development

I built a fully functional, responsive website in **WordPress + Breakdance**, structuring content to improve clarity, engagement and usability.

## Usability Testing

I ran scenario-based usability testing with real users, identified issues, and implemented improvements, for example adding a sorting feature to make trip comparison easier.

## Marketing Thinking

I structured pages around the user journey (awareness → consideration → action), wrote persuasive content, and designed clear calls-to-action and a strong value proposition.

## Results

Delivered a live, responsive website with usability improved through iterative, test-driven design, reducing the effort users needed to make a decision.

## Lessons Learned

Testing with real people beats assumptions every time; the sorting feature came directly from watching users struggle.

## Technologies

Figma, WordPress, Breakdance, UX testing methods. **Live site available.**
