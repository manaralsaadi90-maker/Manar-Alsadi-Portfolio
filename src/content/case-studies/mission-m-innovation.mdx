## Overview

A consumer-research-driven innovation concept that differentiates M&M's from private-label chocolate by turning the product into a **social experience**, a board game inside the packaging to encourage sharing on special occasions. Graded **10/10**.

<Callout>
Role: Research Lead, Consumer Insights Analyst, Concept Developer & Project Coordinator. Student concept project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Mars / M&M's.
</Callout>

## Problem

Private-label chocolate competes hard on price. M&M's needed added value *beyond* the product to justify preference and drive engagement.

## Research

I ran desk research on chocolate trends and social consumption, then designed and distributed consumer surveys to gather both quantitative and qualitative insights, and translated the findings into clear needs, pain points and opportunities.

## Ideation & Concept

Using structured creativity techniques, the team generated and evaluated **100+ ideas**. I proposed the board-game-in-packaging concept, which was selected as our final solution, and built a **Value Proposition Canvas** aligning it to real consumer needs.

## Validation

I defined key business assumptions, designed validation experiments, and tested the concept through surveys, prototype testing and consumer feedback, iterating the prototype based on results.

## Data & Analysis

I analysed purchasing behaviour and survey responses (quantitative + qualitative), identified drivers of brand choice and perceived value, and produced evidence-based recommendations.

## Results

A validated, differentiated innovation concept, a refined prototype grounded in real user insight, and a complete proposal, awarded **10/10**.

## Lessons Learned

Rigorous consumer research turns a nice idea into a defensible one; the 100+ ideas mattered less than the disciplined validation that followed.
