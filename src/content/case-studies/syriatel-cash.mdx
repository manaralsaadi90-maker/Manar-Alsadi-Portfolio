## Overview

As a Product Management Specialist at Syriatel, I managed a portfolio of B2B products and telecom services for corporate clients, from corporate VPN lines to Bulk SMS. Within that role, I spent about two years on **Syriatel Cash**, a mobile e-wallet for bill, government and merchant payments. This case study focuses on that product: growing adoption in a cash-heavy market and expanding the services and partners on the platform.

<Callout>
Role: Product Management Specialist (2018 to 2024). Syriatel Cash was one product within a wider B2B portfolio. I owned assigned service integrations end-to-end and shared responsibility for performance monitoring, reporting and promotion.
</Callout>

## Problem

In a cash-heavy market, the challenge was twofold: convince everyday customers to trust and use a digital wallet, and continuously widen what they could actually do with it so the wallet became part of daily life rather than a one-off experiment.

## Research

We tracked how customers used the service through BI dashboards and transaction data, and listened closely to feedback and complaints to understand where friction and drop-off happened.

## My Role

I owned the delivery of assigned service integrations. For each new service or partner, I coordinated the external company with our internal Marketing, IT and engineering teams, following up until the integration actually worked and went live. Alongside this, I shared responsibility for monitoring performance and reporting.

## Data & Analysis

I pulled raw data from the MIS team and analysed it in advanced Excel and BI dashboards, tracking transactions, adoption and revenue, then translated the findings into reports and recommendations for management about what was working and what to prioritise next.

## Marketing Strategy

We paired B2B partnership deals (bringing new services onto the platform) with B2C promotional campaigns and offers designed to encourage customers to try the wallet and keep using it.

## Development & UX

The developers provided design samples; I reviewed them against real customer feedback and fed back recommendations and modifications to improve the app experience.

## Challenges

The hardest part was coordination under tight launch deadlines, aligning external partner companies with multiple internal teams so a service was ready and working on time.

## Solutions

Clear ownership per integration, disciplined follow-up with every party involved, and constant communication kept launches on schedule.

## Results

Contributed to growing adoption of the wallet and expanding its services over two years. *(In her LinkedIn recommendation, my manager credits my work with helping drive a 45% increase in product revenue.)* I've kept specific company figures private out of respect for confidentiality.

## Lessons Learned

Two big ones: how to balance B2B partnerships with B2C growth so both sides reinforce each other, and how to deliver under real launch-deadline pressure by coordinating internal and external teams toward one date.

## Technologies

BI dashboards, advanced Excel, PowerPoint, CRM, plus close cross-functional work with Marketing, IT and engineering.
