## Overview

The website you're viewing. I conceived the structure and storytelling, defined the content architecture, and directed the build of a modern, production-grade personal site, designed to be fast, accessible, and easy to update.

<Callout>
Role: product owner, designer and content strategist. This was an AI-assisted build: I made the concept, UX, content and design decisions and directed the development, the same way I approached my AI Nutrition Coach and study tools.
</Callout>

## Problem

Most portfolio templates look generic and are hard to maintain. I wanted something that *itself* demonstrates product thinking, modern web literacy and taste, and that I can update without touching code.

## Design Decisions

A calm, premium dark theme with generous whitespace and large typography; purposeful motion (text reveals, parallax, magnetic buttons) that supports the story rather than distracting from it; and a narrative section order that guides visitors from hook to story to proof to contact.

## Content Architecture

All content lives in typed JSON and MDX files, validated automatically. Adding a project, recommendation or certificate means editing a data file or dropping in an image, no component changes.

## Development

Built with **Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lenis** and MDX. Component-based, with reusable UI and animation primitives.

## Performance, SEO & Accessibility

Image optimisation, dynamic fonts and component splitting for speed; full metadata, Open Graph, Twitter cards, JSON-LD structured data, a sitemap and robots for SEO; and keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, semantic HTML and AA-level contrast for accessibility.

## Results

A responsive, animated, SEO-complete portfolio that doubles as evidence of the skills it describes.

## Lessons Learned

Directing a real build clarified how design, content and engineering decisions trade off against each other, and how far one person can go with the right tools.
