## Overview

A data-informed, 360° cross-media marketing strategy for the protein-bar brand Upfront, targeting students and active young consumers to grow awareness, engagement and sales.

<Callout>
Student concept project, the strategy and creative are my academic work, not affiliated with Upfront.
</Callout>

## Research & Analysis

I conducted target-group analysis, applied **DESTEP** and competitor benchmarking, and grounded the work in credible industry sources (Statista, Euromonitor, Nielsen).

## Strategy

I defined USPs and UBRs from the positioning, mapped the full customer journey using **AIDA / STDC** models, and built a 360° cross-media plan spanning online and offline channels with a clear KPI framework across awareness, engagement and conversion.

## Execution & Creative

I designed a social campaign ("Fuel Your Focus") with a TikTok/Instagram content plan and a micro-influencer approach, and produced **AI-generated video ads** plus a marketing poster for the expo presentation, including an A/B-testing approach to creative.

## Results

A complete, audience-aligned 360° strategy with multiple campaign concepts and ad variations, and expo-ready marketing materials demonstrating the frameworks applied in practice.

## Lessons Learned

Frameworks (DESTEP, AIDA/STDC) are only useful when they change the plan, here they sharpened the targeting and the channel mix.

## Technologies

Canva/Adobe, AI video tools (Artlist AI / Sora), Statista/Euromonitor/Nielsen, PowerPoint.
